## 🦷 What is JawAtlas?

JawAtlas is a **free, offline program** that lets you look at 3D dental X-ray pictures (called CBCT scans) right on your own computer. It's made for dental students and teachers, but anyone curious about how a jaw looks in 3D can use it. Best of all, it **doesn't need an internet connection** and has **no hidden costs**.

Think of it like a digital microscope for teeth and bones. You can spin the image, slice it open layer by layer, and even see a flat "panoramic" view like the one your dentist shows you. There's even a special "AR" mode (Augmented Reality) that lets you see the model in your real space using your phone or tablet camera – all without sending your data anywhere.

## 🧭 How to Use JawAtlas (The Basics)

JawAtlas is designed to be intuitive, but here's a quick tour to get you started.

### 🖥️ The Main Screen (Tri-Planar MPR)

When you open a scan, you'll see three square pictures side-by-side. This is called **MPR** (Multi-Planar Reconstruction). It shows the same 3D object from three different angles at once:
- **Top view** (looking down)
- **Front view** (looking from the front)
- **Side view** (looking from the side)

Use your **mouse** to:
- **Scroll** to zoom in and out.
- **Click and drag** to move the slice up/down or left/right.

A small line on each picture shows exactly where the other two pictures are "cutting" through the jaw. This is the exact same tool radiologists use.

### 🦷 The Panoramic View

Click the button that looks like a curved jaw (it says "Panoramic" or "Pano"). This flattens the jaw into a single long, curved picture – the kind you often see printed at the dentist. This is great for seeing all your teeth at once.

### 🕶️ The AR Slice-Through Mode

This is the "wow" feature. If you have a smartphone or tablet:
1.  Look for the AR icon (like a 3D cube).
2.  Click it. The app will show a QR code or a pairing code.
3.  Open the camera on your phone and point it at the code.
4.  Your phone will show a hologram-like 3D model of the jaw that you can move your phone around to explore. It feels like magic, but it's all happening locally on your device – no data is sent anywhere.

## 🧩 Troubleshooting

- **Program won't start:** Make sure you have at least 4 GB of free memory (RAM). Try restarting your computer and running the program again.
- **Images look blurry:** Check that you've zoomed in (using the mouse wheel) and that the slice line is positioned correctly.
- **Can't find the download:** Go back to the GitHub page. Look for a green "Releases" button or a link that says "Latest". Click it to find the download link.
- **The AR mode isn't working:** Make sure your phone and computer are on the same Wi-Fi network (if applicable). Also, allow camera access when your browser asks for it.

## 💡 Tips & Tricks for Learning

- **Start with a full scan:** Find a sample scan online (search for "CBCT sample DICOM") and open it in JawAtlas to practice.
- **Use the arrows:** Your keyboard arrow keys will also move the slices. This is great for precise scrolling.
- **Compare views:** Always look at all three MPR views together before making a judgment – it helps you understand 3D relationships.
- **Use the zoom:** Zoom in on a single tooth to see its root structure clearly.
